About the calculation

  • Apple uses storefront-specific pricing. When you pick a price for a base storefront, Apple generates an equalized price for each of the other storefronts using exchange rates, local taxes and local pricing conventions (for example €44.99 or ¥6,000). This tool shows those Apple prices and never converts the US price with an exchange rate.
  • Customer prices can include taxes. In most storefronts outside the United States and Canada the price a customer sees already contains VAT, GST or a similar tax. Apple removes that tax first and remits it; it is never part of your revenue.
  • Taxes are treated differently across countries. In the United States and Canada sales tax is calculated at checkout and added on top of the price. Some storefronts have no tax deducted at all, and a few (for example the United Kingdom, Italy, Spain, Kenya) carry an additional levy tied to Apple's commission.
  • The commission depends on your agreement and the storefront. The standard rate is 30% and the reduced rate is 15% (App Store Small Business Program, and auto-renewable subscriptions after a subscriber's first year).
  • Developer proceeds are not Customer Price × 85%. The formula is: customer price → remove included tax → tax-exclusive amount → minus Apple's commission on that amount → developer proceeds. For Germany at 19% VAT and 15% commission that is about 71% of the customer price.
  • Apple's Financial Reports are the authority. Settled amounts depend on the exact transaction: your tax category, where your business is based, refunds, withholding taxes and Apple's own settlement exchange rate.
  • This calculator is an estimate. It reproduces the per-unit proceeds App Store Connect reports for a price point, but it is not your financial-report data for a specific transaction.
